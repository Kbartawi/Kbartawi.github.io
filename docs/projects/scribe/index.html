Teams still rewrite meeting notes by hand after calls. Scribe, branded Mahdar, drafts private minutes while keeping attendees informed that notes are being taken.

FEATURES

  1. 01

    Record or capture a meeting with every attendee told at the start.

  2. 02

    Draft meeting minutes in Arabic and English for review.

  3. 03

    Keep notes in a private workspace rather than a public demo.

Examples

  • Produce Arabic and English notes from the same session
