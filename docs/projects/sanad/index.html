Problem

Enterprise support teams need consistent answers across channels without losing the option of a human handoff. Sanad runs AI support agents on company knowledge and passes the conversation to staff when a person is needed.

Users
Enterprise support and service teams
Buyer
Heads of customer support and operations
Market
UAE
Stage
Working prototype

FEATURES

  1. 01

    Answer customers across chat, WhatsApp, the web and email.

  2. 02

    Ground answers in the company’s own knowledge.

  3. 03

    Hand the conversation to a human agent when a person is needed.

Examples

  • Answer a routine support question from company knowledge
  • Continue a WhatsApp or web conversation in Arabic or English
  • Hand a sensitive case to a human agent with context preserved
