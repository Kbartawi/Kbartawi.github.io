WHY IT EXISTS

A problem worth
solving.

After a tenant says yes, leasing teams still need to assemble documents, confirm details and agree the payment schedule. Those open items often remain scattered across WhatsApp threads.

Audience
Dubai leasing teams and brokerages
Model
Team software for the week between a tenant saying yes and registration.
Market
Dubai
Languages
English + Arabic
