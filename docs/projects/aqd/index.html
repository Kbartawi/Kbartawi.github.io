THE PROBLEM

Why it exists.

After a tenant says yes, leasing teams still need to assemble documents, confirm details and agree the payment schedule. Those open items often remain scattered across WhatsApp threads.

THE PRODUCT

What it does.

  1. 01

    Prepare a tenancy pack through a guided workflow.

  2. 02

    See readiness gaps, add suggested owners and prepare a clear handoff brief.

  3. 03

    Plan an annual rent schedule with cheque counts and month-end dates.

  4. 04

    Preview the pack through a PDF and print workflow.

WHERE IT FITS

One clear workflow.

  1. 01

    Collect the tenancy details

  2. 02

    Resolve the open items

  3. 03

    Prepare the close pack

WHAT COMES NEXT

The current focus.

  • Secure team sharing
  • Shared document workflow
  • Feedback from a leasing team pilot

These are areas of work, not release-date commitments.
