WHY IT EXISTS

A problem worth
solving.

Customers switch languages and channels. Support teams need the business context and conversation history in one place so the next person can help without starting again.

Audience
E-commerce brands and customer support teams
Model
A customer experience platform for business teams.
Market
Arabic-speaking markets
Languages
English + Arabic

WHAT IT HELPS YOU DO

  1. 01

    Connect policies, FAQs and product knowledge to customer conversations.

  2. 02

    Support Arabic and English with dialect-aware response planning.

  3. 03

    Manage messaging and web-chat conversations through a shared support workspace.

  4. 04

    Escalate questions that need human judgment and carry the context into the handoff.

Made for moments like these.

  • Answer recurring delivery, returns and product-policy questions for an e-commerce brand
  • Support conversations that move between Arabic and English
  • Hand a difficult request to a human agent with the previous conversation and business context
