THE PROBLEM

Why it exists.

Customers switch languages and channels. Support teams need the business context and conversation history in one place so the next person can help without starting again.

THE PRODUCT

What it does.

  1. 01

    Connect business knowledge to Arabic and English customer conversations.

  2. 02

    Support WhatsApp and web chat, with voice in beta.

  3. 03

    Find conversations that need attention, search loaded records and open the original conversation.

  4. 04

    Prepare an internal handoff draft with the recorded context.

WHERE IT FITS

One clear workflow.

  1. 01

    Bring the business context

  2. 02

    Support the conversation

  3. 03

    Hand over with clarity

WHAT COMES NEXT

The current focus.

  • Broader channel coverage
  • Evidence from customer pilots
