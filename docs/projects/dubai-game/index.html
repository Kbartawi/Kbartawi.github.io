Concept

A game world rooted in Dubai’s districts, landmarks and local stories. The current work explores visual direction and early gameplay systems, with a small playable scene planned as the first development milestone.

Users
Players interested in Dubai and Emirati-led fictional worlds
Buyer
Potential players; commercial model not validated
Market
Dubai-inspired world; intended audience under development
Stage
Preproduction · Art review paused

SCOPE

  1. 01

    Research the locations, cultural details and everyday stories that give Dubai its character.

  2. 02

    Develop a coherent cinematic world through environment and character direction.

  3. 03

    Prototype movement, missions, landmark photography and small encounters in Unreal.

  4. 04

    Review environments for cultural authenticity, visual quality and the experience of playing them.

Examples

  • Explore a neighbourhood through a short character-led mission
  • Discover landmarks through a walking and photography objective
  • Test how local places, movement and encounters fit together in a playable city scene
