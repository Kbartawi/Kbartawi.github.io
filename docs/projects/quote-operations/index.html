THE PROBLEM

Why it exists.

Equipment requirements, pricing questions and commercial approvals arrive in different places. Estimators need a complete request and a clear view of what still needs attention before a quotation goes out.

THE PRODUCT

What it does.

  1. 01

    Prepare equipment quantities, capacities and requirements in one request.

  2. 02

    Build a sourced quotation and keep missing requirements visible.

  3. 03

    Require a separate approver for each quotation version.

  4. 04

    Create a revision without overwriting the approved version. Download HTML and CSV.

WHERE IT FITS

One clear workflow.

  1. 01

    Prepare the request

  2. 02

    Review and approve

  3. 03

    Create the next revision

WHAT COMES NEXT

The current focus.
