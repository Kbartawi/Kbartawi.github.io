THE PROBLEM

Why it exists.

A customer asks for a price. Someone still has to read the equipment list, copy the quantities, find the right products and check the selling prices. When details are missing, the quote goes back to a colleague for a decision.

THE PRODUCT

What it does.

  1. 01

    With AI set up, paste an English or Arabic request from email or WhatsApp, or upload one printed PDF equipment list. The app reads the lines and quantities.

  2. 02

    Review suggested products and available prices beside the request. Open a line to check where its product or price came from.

  3. 03

    See missing quantities, uncertain selections and unavailable prices first. Keep technical decisions with your engineer.

  4. 04

    Confirm the products, prices and terms, then get a colleague’s approval and download the quote. Customer changes get a new version; the earlier quote stays saved.

WHERE IT FITS

One clear workflow.

  1. 01

    Add the request

  2. 02

    Check what needs a decision

  3. 03

    Approve and download

WHAT COMES NEXT

The current focus.

  • Compare one past request with the team’s approved quotation
  • Connect the products, selling prices and approval rules used for that job
  • Measure corrections and preparation time, separately from approval waiting time

These are areas of work, not release-date commitments.
