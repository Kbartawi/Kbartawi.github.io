THE PROBLEM

Why it exists.

A salesperson receives a request by email or WhatsApp, then reads the equipment requirements, searches a catalogue, checks prices and asks colleagues about gaps. That preparation repeats for every quotation, while engineering and commercial decisions still need a person.

THE PRODUCT

What it does.

  1. 01

    Paste an English or Arabic request from email or WhatsApp, or upload one printed PDF. Direct inbox and WhatsApp connections are not part of this pilot.

  2. 02

    Watch the request being read, catalogue items being matched and available prices being applied. Useful results appear as they are ready.

  3. 03

    Review the original request, proposed equipment and price source together. Missing quantities, unavailable prices and engineering questions stay visible.

  4. 04

    Confirm the equipment and commercial terms, then request a separate approval before export. Keep the source and each quotation version.

WHERE IT FITS

One clear workflow.

  1. 01

    Add the request

  2. 02

    Review the prepared draft

  3. 03

    Resolve, approve and export

WHAT COMES NEXT

The current focus.

  • Validate one quotation workflow with an equipment sales team
  • Connect a company-approved catalogue, pricing sources and review rules
  • Measure preparation time and the decisions still waiting on people

These are areas of work, not release-date commitments.
