Problem

During live calls, people need facts quickly without hiding assistance from the other participants. Clarity, branded Lamha, keeps prompts on screen and tells everyone at the start.

Users
People who take live customer or service calls
Buyer
Support and success managers
Market
UAE
Stage
Working prototype

FEATURES

  1. 01

    Show suggested answers and facts on screen during a live conversation.

  2. 02

    Keep a visible sign on, and tell every person on the call at the start.

  3. 03

    Support Arabic and English prompts for the person speaking.

Examples

  • Take a customer call with facts visible on screen
  • Tell every participant that assistance is active
  • Use Arabic or English prompts during the conversation

How it works

  1. 01

    Start the call with notice

    A visible on-call assistant panel

  2. 02

    See prompts on screen

    Suggested answers grounded in prepared facts

  3. 03

    Continue or hand off

    A clear notice that assistance is active
