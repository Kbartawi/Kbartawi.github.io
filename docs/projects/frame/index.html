Privacy and data · Beta support

Problem

The day is split across calendars, task lists, notes and reminders. Frame brings the next realistic action and its supporting information into one private iPhone workspace. It is being built for personal use, with manual planning available while AI and device integrations are validated.

Users
The owner and a small circle of close friends
Buyer
Personal use; no paid plan
Market
Private personal use first
Stage
Private iPhone pilot · Device acceptance pending

FEATURES

  1. 01

    Today and a native day/week plan connect reviewed capture, named editable routines, shared check-offs and Undo; unfinished captures can continue on this device.

  2. 02

    Daily prayers with saved date history, correction/Undo and completion-aware future alerts live beside routines, habits, goals, notes, files and trip checklists.

  3. 03

    Text capture proposes editable next steps; original audio and supported on-device transcription are implemented but phone acceptance is pending.

  4. 04

    Protected local storage, selected-PDF keyword search with exact page links, archive/restore and portable attachment backups. Saved matching excerpts enter chat/backups; exported copies are unencrypted.

  5. 05

    Optional Apple on-device AI requires iOS 27 and eligible, ready hardware in this build. Cloud AI, sync and widgets are not connected.

Examples

  • Bring in a daily-system image, review it and check off a realistic morning
  • Turn a written thought into reviewed next steps
  • Keep personal notes, prayers and trip documents together
  • Choose a PDF, find the original passage and open its exact page
