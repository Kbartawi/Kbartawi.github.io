Privacy and data · Beta support

Concept

Useful decisions get scattered across recordings and notes. Frame is being developed to connect deliberate capture, the original context behind a decision and a user-reviewed next step. The initial audience and repeat-use value remain hypotheses.

Users
Founders and independent professionals with recurring conversations and projects
Buyer
Individual professionals — initial audience hypothesis
Market
Personal use first; UAE audience under validation
Stage
Internal iPhone beta · Device acceptance pending

SCOPE

  1. 01

    Capture a thought or conversation deliberately, with local audio and editable notes.

  2. 02

    Search saved notes, recover supporting passages and return to the original context.

  3. 03

    Review tasks and prepare follow-up drafts; choose reminders and confirm calendar events yourself.

  4. 04

    Keep selected notes out of AI and memory answers, with export and deletion controls.

  5. 05

    Use optional Apple on-device AI on eligible devices, with light, olive-green dark and red night appearance.

Examples

  • Capture a thought before it is forgotten
  • Recall what was agreed in an earlier conversation
  • Prepare a follow-up from a saved note

Technical readiness

An internal iPhone beta with local capture, source-linked recall, reviewed tasks, drafts and manual Calendar handoff. Recording start, pause, resume, save and playback controls were exercised in the iOS 27 simulator. Physical-device acceptance and successful AI generation remain unverified. Optional AI requires an eligible device with iOS 26.4 or later and an enabled, ready Apple model.

Commercial readiness

Private beta available for owner testing. Repeat use and willingness to pay remain unvalidated; no paid launch is available.

  • In progressWorking app flows

    Recording start, pause, resume, save and playback controls passed in the iOS 27 simulator. Manual notes, source navigation, tasks, drafts and Calendar handoff also have simulator evidence. These checks do not establish physical-device reliability.

  • Not checkedVoice and optional AI

    On-device transcription depends on device and language support. Successful AI generation remains unverified: English generation failed in the simulator, with the cause not established; Arabic was unsupported in the tested configuration. Mixed Arabic–English quality remains unverified.

  • In progressPrivacy and control

    Private notes are excluded from AI and memory answers. Sharing, task acceptance, reminders and Calendar additions are deliberate user actions. Physical-device protection checks remain open.

  • VerifiedInternal distribution

    Build 3.0.0 (8) completed Apple processing and is verified in Owner Testing (Internal, one tester), with test instructions saved. Installation and acceptance on the physical iPhone remain unverified.

  • Not checkedCustomer usefulness

    Repeat use and willingness to pay remain unvalidated. The next step is a small observed beta, before charging.

The gallery preserves the 16 original approved design concepts, not proof that every pictured feature is built. Actual simulator screenshots exist separately; physical-device acceptance remains pending.
