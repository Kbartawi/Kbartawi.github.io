Concept

Useful decisions get scattered across recordings and notes. Frame is being developed to connect deliberate capture, the original context behind a decision and a user-reviewed next step. The initial audience and repeat-use value remain hypotheses.

Users
Founders and independent professionals with recurring conversations and projects
Buyer
Individual professionals — initial audience hypothesis
Market
Personal use first; UAE audience under validation
Stage
Internal iPhone beta · Device acceptance pending

SCOPE

  1. 01

    Capture an idea or conversation, then keep the original recording alongside your editable note.

  2. 02

    Find earlier notes and open the source behind a memory answer.

  3. 03

    Review suggested tasks and follow-up drafts before acting.

  4. 04

    Use light, dark or red night appearance, with local data controls.

  5. 05

    Build 5 adds optional local task reminders with generic notification text; its TestFlight availability is not yet verified.

Examples

  • Capture a thought before it is forgotten
  • Recall what was agreed in an earlier conversation
  • Prepare a follow-up from a saved note
