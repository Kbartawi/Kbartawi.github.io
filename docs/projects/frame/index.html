Privacy and data · Beta support

Concept

Useful decisions get scattered across recordings and notes. Frame is being developed to connect deliberate capture, the original context behind a decision and a user-reviewed next step. The initial audience and repeat-use value remain hypotheses.

Users
Founders and independent professionals with recurring conversations and projects
Buyer
Individual professionals — initial audience hypothesis
Market
Personal use first; UAE audience under validation
Stage
Internal iPhone beta · Device acceptance pending

SCOPE

  1. 01

    Capture a thought or conversation deliberately, with local audio and editable notes.

  2. 02

    Search saved notes, recover supporting passages and return to the original context.

  3. 03

    Review tasks and prepare follow-up drafts; choose reminders and confirm calendar events yourself.

  4. 04

    Keep selected notes out of AI and memory answers, with export and deletion controls.

  5. 05

    Optional Apple on-device AI support is being tested on eligible devices. Light, olive-green dark and red night appearance are available.

Examples

  • Capture a thought before it is forgotten
  • Recall what was agreed in an earlier conversation
  • Prepare a follow-up from a saved note

Technical readiness

Build 3.0.0 (14) is verified in internal TestFlight. Local capture, source-linked recall, reviewed tasks, drafts and manual Calendar handoff are implemented. The update improves microphone-permission recovery and local AI retry; its native suite executed 183 checks, with 180 passed and three skipped. Actual simulator recording controls and saved replay controls were exercised. Successful generation, acoustic quality and physical-device acceptance remain unverified. Cloud AI is disabled in Release. Known build14 issue: on a fresh installation, allowing microphone access can immediately pause recording at zero seconds. Tap Resume to continue. Reproduced in a fresh-install simulator workflow; a fix is in progress. Physical-phone cause remains unconfirmed. Build14 is still the delivered internal release.

Commercial readiness

Private beta available for owner testing. Repeat use and willingness to pay remain unvalidated; no paid launch is available.

  • Not checkedCustomer usefulness

    The proposed first audience is people who leave conversations with loose commitments. Repeat use and willingness to pay remain unvalidated. After the device gate, observe a seven-day beta: can people recover context and finish or revise their chosen next step without founder reminders?
