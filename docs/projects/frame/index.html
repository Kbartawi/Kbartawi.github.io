THE PROBLEM

Why it exists.

Making a video means moving between research, scripting, visual generation, narration and editing. An upcoming creator needs a simpler way to direct that process and keep their preferences.

THE PRODUCT

What it does.

  1. 01

    Develop ideas and revise scripts through a saved conversation.

  2. 02

    Use format-specific creative guidance to plan hooks, visual proof and complete payoffs.

  3. 03

    Coordinate a metered video workflow with recorded jobs and explicit review.

  4. 04

    Work toward a finished narrated, captioned film; a successful end-to-end production run remains a validation gate.

WHERE IT FITS

One clear workflow.

  1. 01

    Describe the idea

  2. 02

    Develop the script and scenes

  3. 03

    Generate, review and revise

WHAT COMES NEXT

The current focus.

  • Validate one complete film and the intended voice
  • Verify reliable hosted operation and recovery
  • Test usable video and thumbnail delivery on a phone

These are areas of work, not release-date commitments.
