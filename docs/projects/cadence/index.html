Problem

Appointment requests arrive in chat and email, then get lost before a confirmation goes out. Cadence books the slot and sends confirmations and reminders.

Users
Teams that take appointments
Buyer
Operations managers
Market
UAE
Stage
Working prototype

FEATURES

  1. 01

    Take appointment requests in Arabic and English.

  2. 02

    Place bookings on a shared calendar.

  3. 03

    Send confirmations and reminders to the person who booked.

Examples

  • Book a public or team appointment from a chat request
  • Send a confirmation in Arabic or English
  • Remind the attendee before the visit
