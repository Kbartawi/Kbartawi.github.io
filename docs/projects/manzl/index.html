Problem

Leasing enquiries arrive while agents are busy with viewings. Budget, area, move date and requirements get scattered across conversations, leaving the next agent to reconstruct the brief.

Audience
UAE brokerages and property management teams
Model
Leasing assistance for brokerages and property managers.
Market
UAE
Languages
English + Arabic

FEATURES

  1. 01

    Guide Arabic and English enquiries through the leasing qualification flow.

  2. 02

    Bring budget, location, move date, bedrooms and cheque preferences together.

  3. 03

    Keep missing details and the recorded next action visible beside the conversation.

  4. 04

    Prepare a concise internal handoff and continue into the original lead record.

Examples

  • Qualify a new rental enquiry while a leasing agent is out at viewings
  • Clarify budget and location preferences in an Arabic or English conversation
  • Pass a viewing-ready lead to the leasing desk without reconstructing the chat

How it works

  1. 01

    Receive an enquiry

    A structured tenant brief with budget, area and move-in needs

  2. 02

    Qualify and propose a viewing

    A proposed viewing slot linked to the conversation

  3. 03

    Prepare the agent handoff

    An agent handoff summary with the qualification and next action
