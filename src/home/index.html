The products

Five agents. One standard for public service.

Each product handles one workflow that service teams run every day, from booking an appointment to writing the minutes. Every one is built for Arabic and English, disclosure and human approval.

Cadence

Scheduling

Residents book their appointment online. Your team stops answering the phone to do it.

Cadence is an AI agent that takes public appointments in Arabic and English, around the clock, and sends confirmations and reminders.

  • Booking without a phone call. Residents choose a service, a branch and a time from any phone. The agent checks availability and confirms in seconds.
  • Fewer missed appointments. A confirmation and a reminder by email or WhatsApp, with one tap to move or cancel, so the slot goes to someone else.
  • One view for every branch. Staff see every booking, branch and free slot on one screen.

Apollo

Sales and lead engine

Every request gets a next step, and nobody is forgotten.

Apollo is an AI agent that keeps your team's contacts, cases and follow-ups in one place, and drafts the next message in Arabic or English for your team to approve.

  • One record per contact. Every call, email and request sits on one timeline, so whoever picks it up knows the full story.
  • Follow-ups that happen. The agent lists what is due today and drafts each follow-up. A person approves before anything goes out.
  • A clear weekly view. Managers see open cases, overdue steps and response times without asking for a report.
Request a demo

Clarity

Live meeting assistant

The right answer on your screen during the call, with everyone told.

Clarity is an AI agent that shows facts and suggested answers on your screen while you talk. A visible sign stays on, and every person on the call hears a notice in Arabic and English at the start.

  • Answers while you talk. The policy line, the figure or the next question appears on your screen, so nobody is put on hold.
  • Consent built in. Everyone is told before anything is captured, and anyone can say "stop" at any time.
  • A record you can show. Every notice, agreement and stop is logged per call, so your entity can prove consent to an auditor.
Request a demo

Scribe

Meeting notes

Minutes, decisions and actions ready minutes after the meeting ends.

Scribe is an AI agent that writes meeting minutes in Arabic and English, hosted in the United Arab Emirates, with every attendee told at the start.

  • Minutes without a note-taker. The summary, the decisions and who owns each action, in the language the meeting was held in.
  • A person approves. Every set of minutes stays marked as an AI-generated draft until the chair or secretary approves it.
  • Actions that are tracked. Each action keeps an owner and a due date, so the next meeting starts with what was done.
Request a demo

Sanad

AI customer support

Customer conversations resolved end to end, in Arabic and English, on every channel.

Sanad is an enterprise platform of AI customer support agents. They resolve customer conversations across chat, WhatsApp, the web and email, grounded in your company's own knowledge, and hand off to your human agents when a person is needed.

  • Resolution, not deflection. The agent answers the question and completes the request, so customers get an outcome instead of a link to a help page.
  • Grounded in your knowledge. Answers come from your own policies, help content and customer context, so every reply is accurate and on brand.
  • Seamless human handoff. When a conversation needs a person, your team receives the full history and context, so the customer never repeats themselves.
